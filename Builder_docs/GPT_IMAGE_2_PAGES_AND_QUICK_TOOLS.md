Six application themes × 17 destinations = **102 images**.

## How to copy a complete prompt

For one image, copy **one complete theme block** followed immediately by **one
destination block**. Together they are complete GPT Image 2 prompt: style,
composition, page purpose, output size, and exclusions are all specified. Do
not add text, UI, logos, borders, or a character portrait.
